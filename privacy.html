Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy applies to the small tools on this site, including the file tool that connects to Google Drive. They are personal learning projects, used only by their owner and not offered as a public service.

What Google data is used

The file tool asks for the Google Drive drive.file permission only. With it, the tool can create files in the owner's own Google Drive and see only the files it created itself.

It cannot open, change, share or delete any other files in Google Drive. It does not access email, contacts, calendar, photos or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Files are copied to the owner's own Google Drive for safekeeping. Nothing else is done with them.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or sent to anyone else. No advertising or tracking is used.

Storage and security

The sign-in token is kept only on the owner's own computer and is not published anywhere. Files stay in the owner's Google Drive and are protected by Google's own security.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the tool can no longer reach Google Drive.
