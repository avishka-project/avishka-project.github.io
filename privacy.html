Privacy policy

GreenThru Backup is used only by its owner to copy backup files of the GreenThru Label System to the owner's own Google Drive.

It uses the Google Drive "drive.file" permission: it can only create files and see the files it created itself. It does not read, share, or delete any other Drive files, and it does not access email, contacts, or any other Google data.

No data is sent to anyone else. Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
